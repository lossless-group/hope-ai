# The weekly form: drafting ChoiceCenter's weekly PSP roadmap

Each week, every member submits the program's weekly PSP roadmap (a Google Form,
shared with their accountability buddy). It's due **Sunday afternoon**. This file
maps every question to where its answer comes from, so the Sunday session can
draft most of it from the log.

Captured 2026-10-04 from one cohort's form. Coaches may vary the wording; if a
member pastes a different version, follow theirs.

## Rules

- **Draft, never submit.** The member reviews and pastes every answer. Never fill
  in or submit the form for them.
- **Use the form's exact wording and option text.** A dropdown answer must match
  the option as written, even where the option has a typo or an error.
- **Honest over passing.** "On track?" and "Is your declaration 10–15% of your
  goal?" are answered from the data. If the answer is No, draft No and say why.
- **Celebrate, don't score.** The form asks for results to celebrate. Turn hits
  into human sentences ("Three gym sessions, two of them before 8 am"), not
  status labels.
- **Other people's names are private.** Loved ones, enrolling conversations,
  team members, and the buddy's email live in the member's `private/` folder, never
  in a shared dashboard, a public repo, or an example. Use only what the member
  gives you, and only in the draft.

## The goals on the form: 3 + 1 + 1

The program's plan has five goals: three **Personal** goals (any area), a fourth
that is **Creative**, and a fifth that is always **Relationships**. Page 2 of the
form carries goals 1 to 4. The Relationships goal is what page 3 (enrolling
conversations) and page 1's loved-ones questions are about. In `tracker.yaml`,
`kind: Personal | Creative | Relationships` says which is which.

## Page 1: results

| Question | Draft from |
|---|---|
| Email · Name · Buddy's email | Leave blank; the member fills these in. |
| Week # (1–12) | The program week from `day_one` and `week_starts` (see [[references/Weekly-Cadence.md]]). Confirm it matches how their coach counts. |
| Celebrate your top 3 results from last week (#1, #2, #3) | The week's biggest wins: stretch hits, bonus habits done, a hard week where base still held, progress on an outcome, something they shipped. Offer 4–5 candidates; they pick 3. |
| My breakthrough learnings last week | Theirs to write. Offer the patterns you saw (what made base easy or hard), then ask. |
| Did you enroll a loved one last week? YES / NO | From the log, if they track it; otherwise ask. |
| If YES, celebrate who they are to you and what you see possible for them | Theirs to write; prompt them. |
| Total loved ones enrolled to date | Running count, from the previous week's saved answer plus this week. |

## Page 2: PSP goals (goals 1–4)

For each goal (Personal 1, 2, 3, then the Creative goal):

| Question | Draft from |
|---|---|
| PSP 100-Day Goal | The goal's one-line title, the same every week. |
| Am I currently on track or off track for my 100 days? (asked once, under Goal #1) | The whole plan's process pace across all goals, not Goal #1 alone. Off track if the habits are behind pace; mention any blocker and its logged reason. |
| Declaration for this week (specific actions I am taking) | Next week's base and stretch from the ramp, written as specific actions: "3 resistance sessions, one 36-hour fast, keto until dinner 5 of 7." Pull in that week's row of the weekly action plan. |
| By When (Day and Time) | Suggest one (e.g. Saturday 8 pm, the end of the cohort week); the member decides. |
| Is your weekly declaration 10–15% of your overall goal? | Compute it: this week's stretch ÷ the 100-day total for the habit, or this week's share of the outcome's distance. Ramps start low and deload in Weeks 8 and 12, so early and deload weeks often honestly read No. Say the number, then let them answer. |

## Page 3: enrolling conversations

Three are required each week, five allowed.

| Question | Draft from |
|---|---|
| Name and Relationship to Me (1–5) | Their private list: offer last week's unfinished conversations and anyone they've mentioned as likely interested. |
| Activity and By When? | Theirs to choose; suggest a concrete next step and a day. |

Members often find this page the most tedious. Keep it light: carry names
forward, offer the list, don't push for more.

## Page 4: program practices

| Question | Draft from |
|---|---|
| Which Daily Practices did you complete in excellence last week? (Daily Journaling · Daily Meditation · Gratitude Walk, 40 minutes total for the week) | The log, if these are tracked as habits. Offer the boxes the log supports; "in excellence" is the member's call, so ask before ticking a partial week. |
| Did you complete a Book this week? Yes / No | The log, or ask. |
| If yes, which Book did you complete? | A dropdown; answer with the option text exactly (list below). |
| Buddy Accountability: Did you support your Buddy to be on Track last week? Yes / No | The log (buddy calls), or ask. |
| Who will you make a difference with on your team this week? | A choice of the cohort's team members. Rotate through the list; names stay private. |

## What to save

After they submit, save the final answers as `weekly-form/week-NN.md` in their PSP
folder (names in `private/`). Next Sunday starts from it.
